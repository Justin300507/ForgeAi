An IT capacity planning system with user login. Admins track resource pools (servers, software licenses, headcount) with current capacity and monthly utilization records. They also enter demand forecasts per resource for the next 12 months. The system projects when each resource will exceed 80% utilization based on the forecast trend and surfaces an alert with the projected breach date. A what-if simulator lets planners test the effect of adding N units of a resource on the breach timeline.

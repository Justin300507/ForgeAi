An internal audit management system with user login. Audit leads create audit programs with scope and objectives, then assign fieldwork tasks to auditors. Auditors log findings with title, severity (critical/major/minor/observation), and management response. Each finding has a remediation action with owner and target date. Findings are tracked through open → in-progress → remediated → closed (confirmed by audit lead). The dashboard shows open findings by severity and overdue remediations.

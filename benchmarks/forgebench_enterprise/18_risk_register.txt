An enterprise risk register with user login. Risk owners log risks with title, description, category, likelihood (1–5), impact (1–5), and treatment plan (accept/mitigate/transfer/avoid). The system calculates a risk score (likelihood × impact) and displays a heat-map grid. Risks above the defined appetite threshold are flagged red. Quarterly review workflow: owners must update their risks every 90 days or they are escalated to management.

A subscription billing platform with mock payments (no real Stripe calls). Users choose a plan: Free ($0/month, 5 projects), Pro ($29/month, unlimited projects), Team ($99/month, 10 seats). "Payment" is simulated by storing a payment record with card_last4 (hardcoded mock) and setting subscription status to active. Users can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel. Cancel sets subscription to end at period end. Invoice history shows past payments.

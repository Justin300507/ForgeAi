A restaurant point-of-sale system with staff and manager roles. Managers create the menu: categories (starters/mains/desserts/drinks) and items (name, price, available toggle). Staff open tables, add items to the table's order, and mark items as served. Multiple items can be added in one order. Staff generate a bill (itemized total + tax at 10%). Manager sees daily sales report: total revenue, number of orders, top-selling items. Tables can be cleared after payment.
